Questions

Frequently asked

How long does a website take?

A Starter site is usually ready in 5 days and a Business site in about 10 days, once we have your photos and text. Premium sites and apps can take longer, and you get a clear timeline before work starts.

Do I need to provide photos and text?

Your own photos and words make the site feel real, so they are best. If you don't have them yet, we can help write the text and use suitable images to get you started.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Most visitors in the hills browse on their phones, so every site is designed for mobile first and then for tablets and laptops.

What about domain and hosting?

We can register your domain and set up hosting for you. This usually costs ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 a year, paid separately from the design fee.

Can I update the website myself?

Simple sites are built so they are easy to change. Bigger sites can include an admin panel, like the reporter panel on Khabar Darjeeling. Help with updates is always available.

Do you work with clients outside Darjeeling?

Yes. We work with clients in Kalimpong, Kurseong, Siliguri, Sikkim and anywhere else. Everything can be handled over WhatsApp, calls and email.
